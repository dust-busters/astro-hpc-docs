# Overview

The group has five compute nodes, reachable through the INDACO login node. They come in two flavours: `astro01–03` have more cores, `astro04–05` have more memory and a higher clock.

| Node      | RAM    | CPU cores | Max clock | CPU model     | Arch   |
|-----------|--------|-----------|-----------|---------------|--------|
| `astro01` | 256 GB | 2 × 24    | 3.6 GHz   | AMD EPYC 7413 | x86_64 |
| `astro02` | 256 GB | 2 × 24    | 3.6 GHz   | AMD EPYC 7413 | x86_64 |
| `astro03` | 256 GB | 2 × 24    | 3.6 GHz   | AMD EPYC 7413 | x86_64 |
| `astro04` | 512 GB | 2 × 16    | 3.9 GHz   | AMD EPYC 7343 | x86_64 |
| `astro05` | 512 GB | 2 × 16    | 3.9 GHz   | AMD EPYC 7343 | x86_64 |
